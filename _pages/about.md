I am an MS student and Research Assistant at the [Graphics Realization Lab (GRLab)](https://grlab.cau.ac.kr/), Chung-Ang University, Seoul, Republic of Korea. My research focuses on computer vision, deep learning, multimodal AI, and vision-language models, with applications in fire and smoke analysis, disaster understanding, segmentation, affective computing, medical AI, and multimodal reasoning. I previously worked at the Digital Image Processing Laboratory, Islamia College University, Pakistan, and my broader interests include Generative AI, Large Language Models, representation learning, and deployable intelligent systems.
