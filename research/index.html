Research

Research directions and active projects in computer vision and multimodal artificial intelligence.

My work centers on robust visual and multimodal systems for safety-critical, affective, medical, and disaster-response applications. Published results, manuscripts under review, and ongoing projects are labeled separately throughout this site.

Fire and Smoke Understanding

Fire and smoke detection, instance and semantic segmentation, weakly supervised learning, frequency-aware representations, adaptive feature fusion, and edge deployment across challenging indoor and outdoor scenes.

Featured work: BoXFire (published) and DPAF-Net (under review).

Industrial Gas and Fire Monitoring

TLD-Net is an Edge-AIoT framework for unified industrial gas-leak and fire monitoring, with an emphasis on visual perception, industrial safety, and deployable edge inference.

Under Review

Multimodal Emotion Understanding

Combining vision, audio, and language for affective computing, cross-modal reasoning, uncertainty-aware learning, and emotion understanding.

In Progress

Disaster Vision-Language Models

Developing a disaster image dataset and VLM framework spanning fire, flood, earthquake, collapsed-building, and normal scenes. The project studies recognition, scene context, severity estimation, multimodal reasoning, response-oriented decision support, and VLM evaluation.

Research Project · In Progress

Medical Vision-Language Models

Medical VQA, report understanding and generation, and resource-efficient vision-language adaptation for X-ray, CT, and MRI data.

Featured work: MedVLM (published).

Video Understanding

Prior work and research participation involving anomaly detection, action recognition, multimodal violence detection, CNN-LSTM architectures, and video understanding.

Autonomous Driving

Research participation involving the ROAD-R Challenge for NeurIPS 2023, Formula Student cone detection, FSOCO, road-crack annotation, and KITTI 3D object detection.
